How to Play

Move WASD or arrows   Jump Space (press again in the air to double jump)
Ground pound C or Shift in the air   Fireball F or J
Camera drag the mouse, or Q and E   Pause Esc or P

Stomp enemies from above. Spiky ones need a fireball or a star. Grab flags to set checkpoints, find the 3 Star Shards in every level, and defeat the boss at the end of each world by stomping its head. Collect 100 shards to open the Space World.
