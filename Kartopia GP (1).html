Battle Mode

Every kart starts with balloons. Hit rivals with shells, bombs, oil and stars to pop theirs. The last kart standing wins, or whoever has the most balloons when time runs out.

Hold Shift or Space while steering to start a drift. The longer you hold it, the bigger the turbo when you let go (blue, orange, then pink). Steering assist gently straightens the kart when you are not turning and can be switched off in the pause menu. Some tracks run counter-clockwise and the figure 8 tracks cross over themselves on a bridge. In Battle Mode you pop rivals' balloons with items; the last kart standing wins. Stretches marked in red on the minimap have no guardrail, so keep to the road. Oil puddles shove you sideways out of the way. Easy, Medium and Hard change how fast and sharp the CPU racers are, and they speed up when they fall behind you. Weather can be switched on or off in the main menu or while paused: rain, snow, fog and storms make the road slipperier. Chevron pads give a speed boost. Ramps and spring discs launch you into the air; some ramps lead over open gaps, so keep your speed up. Rainbow orbs hold items. Blue pit pads grant a shield, a boost and a spare item. Dark oil puddles spin you out. If you fall off the road you are placed back on track.
