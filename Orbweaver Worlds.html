How to Play

Move WASD   Camera drag mouse, arrow keys or Q/E
Jump Space, press again in the air for a double jump
Spin J or F: hits enemies and crystals, slows your fall, throws fire with a Fire Flower
Dive Shift or C: dive from the air, or slide on the ground. Jump out of a slide for a long leap
Talk E near a villager   Pause / orb list Esc or P

Every world hides 8 Magic Orbs: four villager missions, a coin collection, a sky spire, an island hop and the Guardian. Get 24 orbs in a set of four worlds to open the next set. Beat all 12 Guardians to open world 13, the Cosmic Rift.
